# CheeseCave branding

CheeseCave is the name of this self-hosted service. It is an independent fork of KohakuHub,
and the attribution to KohakuHub, its authors and the DeepGHS fork stays in NOTICE.md, the
about page and the license files.

## Names

- **CheeseCave** names the service, the three repositories, the container images
  (`ghcr.io/cheesecave/*`) and the PyPI names `cheesecave` and `cheesecave-backend`.
- **KohakuHub** is used only to describe the origin of the code, never as the product name.
- The Python import package remains `kohakuhub`, and `KOHAKU_HUB_*` settings remain as they are;
  they are compatibility identifiers, not branding.
- Administrators may set their own display name and logo at runtime. That choice takes precedence
  over the defaults in this repository.

## Logo and mascot

No mascot or logo artwork is shipped yet. Until one is designed, use the plain CheeseCave
wordmark and do not invent artwork that could be mistaken for an official one.

## Forks

A fork that is deployed to other people should not present itself as the official CheeseCave
service unless it is published from the `cheesecave` organization.
